    What the material types mean

    Blanket is the average of the four blankets tested so far (a woven throw, two fuzzy fleeces and an open-knit fleecy throw): the geometric mean of their per-layer protection factors, pressure drops and thicknesses. Real blankets vary a lot either side of it.

    Duvet is a tested 13.5 tog duvet with synthetic polyester fill. Down and feather duvets have not been tested and may behave quite differently.

    Linen is a tested polyester soft-touch duvet cover.

    Protection factors are for 0.3 µm particles, counted, from whole-roll tests.
